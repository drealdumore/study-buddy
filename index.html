Who can use StudyBuddy?

Students at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. Anyone can browse after signing up, and a verified badge unlocks connecting, sessions and chats.

How does verification work?

You upload your course registration slip once. A real person reviews it, usually within 1 to 2 days, and you get a notification with the result.

What happens to my documents?

They are stored privately, only reviewers can open them, and they are deleted within 30 days of a final decision.

Can I use the app before I am verified?

You can browse course mates and sessions. Sending requests, joining sessions and chatting unlock after verification.

Is StudyBuddy affiliated with ABU?

No. StudyBuddy is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ahmadu Bello University.

FAQ

Questions
answered.

The short answers. For anything else, contact us.

Who can use StudyBuddy?

Students at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. Anyone can browse after signing up, and a verified badge unlocks connecting, sessions and chats.

How does verification work?

You upload your course registration slip once. A real person reviews it, usually within 1 to 2 days, and you get a notification with the result.

What happens to my documents?

They are stored privately, only reviewers can open them, and they are deleted within 30 days of a final decision.

Can I use the app before I am verified?

You can browse course mates and sessions. Sending requests, joining sessions and chatting unlock after verification.

Is StudyBuddy affiliated with ABU?

No. StudyBuddy is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ahmadu Bello University.

How are people matched?

By the courses you register. You see students who share at least one of your courses.

Can I block or report someone?

Yes. Open the chat menu to mute, block or report a person. Reports go to reviewers.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings, then Delete account. You will be asked to confirm. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Which phones does it run on?

StudyBuddy is built for iOS and Android.

How do I report a problem?

Use the contact page or the Report a problem link in the app settings.
